1. **Introduction (150 words)**
   - Overview of the current political climate in India.
   - *Keyword guidance:* Mention primary keyword "latest politics news India" once in the first 100 words.

2. **National Assembly & Parliament Updates (300 words) [H2]**
   - Summary of current bills, debates, and legislative action.
   - *Format:* Live updates table or summary timeline of bills.
   - *Keyword guidance:* Use "Indian polity news" in H3.

3. **State Elections & Electoral Maps (400 words) [H2]**
   - State-by-state breakdown of active or upcoming elections.

   - *FS Target:* "Who is eligible to vote in state elections in India?" (Definition box)

4. **Alliances and Coalition Dynamics (400 words) [H2]**
   - In-depth look at major coalitions (NDA, I.N.D.I.A.) and key regional parties.
   - *Keyword guidance:* Use "elections in India live updates" in H3.

5. **Policy Analysis & Public Response (300 words) [H2]**
   - Analysis of key economic and social policies and public reaction.

6. **Conclusion & News Alerts (150 words) [H2]**
   - Recap of key dates to watch. Call to subscribe to newsletter.
