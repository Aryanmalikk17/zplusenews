## Content Brief: Latest Business News India

### Search Intent
- **Intent Type:** Informational / Commercial
- **SERP Format:** Stock market ticker charts, business news lists, economic indices.
- **Target Audience:** Business owners, startup founders, retail investors, corporate workers. Knowledge level is intermediate.

### Content Gaps and Opportunities
- **Topic Gaps:** Plain English breakdown of inflation indicators and how they impact local retail and lending.
- **Depth Gaps:** Clear funding round summaries for non-unicorn startups doing innovative work.
- **Quality Gaps:** Cluttered stock boards; ZPluse News can win with clean, modern data summaries and responsive layout.

### Winning Outline

**H1:** Latest Business News India: Stock Market Trends, Inflation & Startup Funding
**URL Slug:** /category/economics
**Target Word Count:** ~1,800 words (competitor avg: ~1,400 words)

#### Outline Structure:
1. **Introduction (150 words)**
   - Summary of current macroeconomic highlights in India.
   - *Keyword guidance:* Use "latest business news India" in the first 100 words.

2. **Stock Market Trends & Live Indicators (350 words) [H2]**
   - Analysis of Nifty, Sensex, and key sector performances (Tech, Banking, Auto).
   - *Format:* Clean visual table showing sector leaders and laggards.

3. **Indian Startup Ecosystem & Funding News (400 words) [H2]**
   - Active VC rounds, funding announcements, and growth stories.
   - *Keyword guidance:* Use secondary keyword "Indian startup news" in H3.
   - *Format:* Infobox listing top 5 funded startups of the week.

4. **Inflation, Policy Rates & Economic Trends (400 words) [H2]**
   - Overview of RBI policy decisions, interest rates, and retail inflation stats.
   - *Keyword guidance:* Use secondary keyword "Union Budget of India news" in H3 when discussing state budget reviews.

5. **Industry Focus: Tech & Manufacturing (350 words) [H2]**
   - Production Linked Incentive (PLI) updates, EV expansion, and local production.

6. **Summary & Financial Calendar (150 words) [H2]**
   - Key dates for upcoming earnings or economic updates.
